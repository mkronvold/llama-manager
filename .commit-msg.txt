Fix canvas background-color bleed and flaky GPU counter dropouts

- drawBar() in SystemTab and the Ctx-usage bar in MetricsPanel used
  fgBg() to paint colored segments but never reset the canvas
  background afterward. FramebufferCanvas background state is
  persistent (set via setBackgroundColor), so it leaked into
  subsequent plain fg() writes: observed as a red background bleeding
  from a 100%-filled CPU bar into its own label and the next row
  (e.g. "RAM"). Both call sites now reset the background to "None"
  immediately after drawing.
- getWindowsCounterGpuSnapshots() now retries once (after a short
  delay) when Get-Counter transiently reports zero GPU adapters,
  instead of immediately falling back and causing the System tab's
  displayed source/GPU count to flap between refreshes.
